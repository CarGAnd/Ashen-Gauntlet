# Contestant Field Matrix

Status: Working placement-facing consolidation of the 25 registered Games entries. This document consolidates the player party, primary rival slots A-J, and background rival slots K-X without assigning final NPC identities, gem colors, or exact insertion anchors.

## Purpose

This matrix is intended to support three later tasks: distributing the 25 teams across the seven proposed Jungle Ring territories, checking early encounter-sphere difficulty, and giving the arena simulation enough mechanical information to judge route suitability.

The seven current Map Designer territory proposals are:
- JR-A Raincoil Basin: wet low jungle; BK/GW/VE access.
- JR-B Broken Canopy: elevated giant-tree shelves; VE/DR/VC access.
- JR-C Blackwater Steps: ravines/cataracts; BK/DR/GW access.
- JR-D Emberridge: ash ridge/fumaroles; DR/VC plus limited LA access.
- JR-E Cloudfang Escarpment: high cliffs/mist forest; VE/DR plus limited VD access.
- JR-F Ashfall Terraces: lava terraces/talus; GW/VC/VE access.
- JR-G Splitroot Ravines: chasms/root bridges; BK/GW plus limited SH access.

These territories remain Map Designer proposals pending cross-project acceptance. The matrix therefore expresses suitability rather than permanent placement.

## Rating shorthand

- Combat L5/L8: relative direct-combat threat to the four-character player party at that player level.
- Magic: None, Low, Moderate, High, or Very High.
- Recovery: Poor, Limited, Moderate, Strong, or Very Strong.
- Scout: Poor, Moderate, Strong, or Exceptional.
- Mobility: Ordinary, Good, High, or Exceptional.
- Fidelity: Player, Primary, or Background.

## Territory balancing guidance

The placement pass should use the matrix to balance *encounter spheres*, not simply distribute strong teams evenly across the seven territories.

Recommended principles:
- Each territory should normally contain at least one Peer-or-better rival and at least one Inferior-or-specialist rival at player level 5.
- No player starting feeder should contain two Overmatch entries unless there is strong geographic separation and an obvious noncombat escape route.
- Do not place both of the returning entries D and U in the same territory; prior Games knowledge should remain geographically dispersed.
- Do not cluster all high-mobility entries J, F, G, and V into the high/cliff territories; some should begin in terrain that only partially suits them.
- Do not cluster all strong magical entries B, L, W, and X around the limited strange-route territories JR-D and JR-E.
- Large entries H and Q need enough physical space to function, but should not both receive the safest broad-road starts.
- The weaker entries should not all be sacrificed into the same dangerous territory. They need plausible routes by which some may survive into the mixing phase.
- Exceptional Jungle seeds LA-D1, VD-E1, and SH-G1 are discoveries, not placement rewards. A team beginning in JR-D/E/G should not automatically know or exploit them.

## Player insertion requirements

Until the actual player classes are known, treat the player party as a four-person level-5 generalist with unknown specialization.

A good player territory should provide:
- Three ordinary macro-route choices at or shortly after the local hub where possible.
- At least two mechanically different route families so one missing party capability does not create a false choice.
- One optional reward/dead-end or information opportunity before full breakout.
- A local rival sphere containing 2-3 other teams with different mechanical profiles.
- At least one rival that is plausibly negotiable/avoidable because raw combat is unfavorable or unnecessary.
- No starting geometry that falsely rewards apparent altitude with uniquely short inner access.
- No mandatory early fight against an Overmatch entry.

The player insertion anchor itself should not be materially better than rival anchors. Player agency should come from choices made after insertion rather than privileged topology.

## Information needed before exact placement

Exact placement should wait until:
- The Map Designer's seven-territory topology is accepted or revised.
- The player party's actual level-5 class composition is known well enough to identify severe capability gaps.
- The Campaign Architect or Project Lead approves the mechanical contestant field.
- Gem colors are distributed separately under the existing constrained-random rules after team placement, rather than using gem color to justify a desired rival encounter.

When those inputs exist, the next artifact should be a 25-row insertion assignment table: team entry, territory, exact insertion anchor, local feeder, starting gem color, and early plausible rival sphere.
