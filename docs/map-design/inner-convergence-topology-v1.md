# The Ashen Gauntlet: Inner Convergence Topology V1

Status: Accepted project baseline for campaign topology. Geographic display names remain provisional and may be renamed during scene design without architectural review.

## Purpose

This document defines the network-mastery and convergence layer of the volcano: the Veiled Domain, Labyrinth of Ash, Ember Kingdoms, Shattered Heart, Caldera Underworld, and wilderness-facing outer Crucible thresholds.

It consumes the reserved interfaces from the first interior-mixing topology and integrates the exceptional Jungle seeds `VD-E1`, `LA-D1`, and `SH-G1`.

It does not design the Prime Victor procedure, protected Crucible interior, detailed encounters, or scene-scale battlemaps.

The central design requirement is controlled convergence: contestant populations should collide more often, but no single Shattered Heart node, Caldera node, or threshold may control the Games.## Inner-Network Structural Model

The inner game uses five different network behaviors rather than a single funnel:

- **Veiled Domain:** three geometrically linked sanctum hubs; route activation and information matter more than physical adjacency.
- **Labyrinth of Ash:** three unstable memory nodes; useful for lateral shortcuts, but deliberately not a primary progression spine.
- **Ember Kingdoms:** three living political/thermal districts; a major alternative route toward the Caldera that does not require the Shattered Heart.
- **Shattered Heart:** four fault sectors forming the broadest collision mesh; many histories enter here, but no single sector is mandatory.
- **Caldera Underworld:** three final wilderness sectors, each feeding an independent outer Crucible threshold.

The final state therefore narrows macro-direction without collapsing route diversity. Teams can arrive through engineered, ecological, elemental, magical, social, or fault-route histories and still choose among several final approaches.## Veiled Domain

The Veiled Domain is organized around three sanctum hubs rather than a physical corridor.

| Hub | Working name | Main incoming routes | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `VD-H1` | Root Meridian | `VD-BK1`, `VD-VE1`, `VD-DR1` | lower geomantic junction |
| `VD-H2` | Glass Observatory | `VD-VE2`, `VD-GW1`, exceptional `VD-E1` | knowledge / alignment hub |
| `VD-H3` | Starfold Axis | `VD-DR2`, `VD-VC1` | upper activated connector |

Exceptional Jungle seed `VD-E1` must first cross `VD-XE1` Blind Meridian before reaching `VD-H2`.

Internal links: `VD-H1 <-> VD-H2`, `VD-H2 <-> VD-H3`, plus conditional `VD-H1 <-> VD-H3` after understanding or activating the local geometry.

Primary exits to the Labyrinth use two separate interfaces from `VD-H1` and `VD-H3`. Primary exits to the Shattered Heart use separate interfaces from `VD-H2` and `VD-H3`. A limited route from `VD-H3` reaches the Ember Kingdoms, and another limited upper route reaches the Caldera.## Labyrinth of Ash

The Labyrinth is a strange lateral network rather than a compulsory route toward victory.

| Hub | Working name | Main incoming routes | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `LA-H1` | Echo Cloister | `LA-BK1`, `LA-VD1`, exceptional `LA-D1` | damaged memory threshold |
| `LA-H2` | Returning Gallery | `LA-VE1`, `LA-VD2` | repeat-route and time-slip junction |
| `LA-H3` | Ash Clock | `LA-DR1` | unstable deep/upper cross-layer node |

Exceptional Jungle seed `LA-D1` first crosses `LA-XD1` False Arrival before reaching `LA-H1`.

Internal links are intentionally unreliable: `LA-H1 <-> LA-H2` is perception-dependent, `LA-H2 <-> LA-H3` is stable enough to learn, and `LA-H3 -> LA-H1` can behave as a directional memory shortcut.

The Labyrinth has one limited Shattered Heart breach from `LA-H3` and one limited Caldera route from `LA-H2`. It has no direct routes to the Great Warrens, Ember Kingdoms, or Vent-Cities, preserving the canonical matrix.## Ember Kingdoms

The Ember Kingdoms form the strongest non-Shattered-Heart route toward the Caldera. They remain living political geography rather than a simple lava dungeon.

| Hub | Working name | Main incoming routes | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `EK-H1` | Basalt Concord | `EK-DR1`, `EK-GW1`, `EK-VC1` | lower trade / treaty junction |
| `EK-H2` | Chainforge Reach | `EK-DR2`, `EK-GW2` | industrial and military heat corridor |
| `EK-H3` | Ember Stair | `EK-VC2`, `EK-VE1`, `EK-VD1` | rising inner route toward upper volcanic systems |

Internal links: `EK-H1 <-> EK-H2`, `EK-H2 <-> EK-H3`, and a conditional diplomatic/tribute route `EK-H1 <-> EK-H3`.

Two primary routes enter the Shattered Heart, from `EK-H1` and `EK-H3`. Two primary routes enter the Caldera, from `EK-H2` and `EK-H3`.

This gives teams a real choice between entering the collision-heavy Shattered Heart and remaining within elemental society long enough to approach the final mountain from a different direction.## Shattered Heart

The Shattered Heart is the broad late-game collision region, but it is a mesh rather than a hub-and-spoke funnel.

| Hub | Working name | Typical incoming families | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SH-H1` | Broken Aquifer | BK/VE/GW/DR plus exceptional Jungle seed | outer low fault sector |
| `SH-H2` | Suspended Ward | BK/VC/VD | mixed infrastructure fault sector |
| `SH-H3` | Red Rift | VE/GW/EK | inner thermal collision sector |
| `SH-H4` | Crown Fracture | DR/VC/VD/EK/LA | upper fault sector nearest final approaches |

Exceptional Jungle seed `SH-G1` crosses `SH-XG1` Split Scar and `SH-XG2` Hanging Fault before reaching `SH-H1`; it therefore discovers the Shattered Heart early without skipping its local traversal.

Internal links form a resilient mesh: `SH-H1 <-> SH-H2`, `SH-H2 <-> SH-H3`, `SH-H3 <-> SH-H4`, dangerous `SH-H1 <-> SH-H3`, and unstable `SH-H2 <-> SH-H4`.

Three separate primary interfaces leave the Heart for the Caldera from `SH-H2`, `SH-H3`, and `SH-H4`. A limited upper scar from `SH-H4` also supports the canonical exceptional Shattered-Heart-to-Crucible route.## Caldera Underworld

The Caldera Underworld is the final wild network before organiser control. It uses three mutually connected sectors rather than one staging chamber.

| Hub | Working name | Main incoming families | Final role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CU-H1` | Furnace Rim | GW/LA/EK/SH | western/deep final sector |
| `CU-H2` | Obsidian Crown | VC/EK/SH | central thermal final sector |
| `CU-H3` | Steam Crown | VE/DR/VD/SH | high fractured final sector |

Primary and limited landings enter these sectors through distinct nodes: `CU-EK1`, `CU-EK2`, `CU-SH1`, `CU-SH2`, `CU-SH3`, `CU-VE1`, `CU-DR1`, `CU-GW1`, `CU-VC1`, `CU-VD1`, and `CU-LA1`.

The three hubs form a triangle: `CU-H1 <-> CU-H2`, `CU-H2 <-> CU-H3`, and `CU-H3 <-> CU-H1`. These lateral routes are dangerous but ordinary enough that a team discovering one final sector is not permanently committed to its threshold.

Each hub has its own threshold approach node: `CU-T1` Furnace Causeway, `CU-T2` Crater Teeth, and `CU-T3` Ash Crown Stair. These lead independently to three wilderness-facing outer Crucible thresholds.## Outer Crucible Threshold Network

The wilderness-facing threshold is part of the Gauntlet; protected organiser territory begins only beyond successful qualification.

Three equivalent outer threshold sectors are established:
- `CR-O1` Furnace Gate, reached normally from `CU-T1`;
- `CR-O2` Crater Gate, reached normally from `CU-T2`;
- `CR-O3` Crown Gate, reached normally from `CU-T3`.

None is mechanically superior by default. Each exposes the same visible five-color qualification requirement and each remains dangerous wilderness-side ground until a team successfully crosses the protected boundary.

The five Closing Ring gem-recovery shrines use `CR-S1` through `CR-S5`. They form a wilderness-facing reliquary circuit: `S1-S2-S3-S4-S5-S1`. `CR-O1` attaches near `S1/S2`, `CR-O2` near `S3`, and `CR-O3` near `S4/S5`.

This makes every color shrine contestable from every final approach without making the shrine circuit mandatory for ordinary qualification. The exact gem-color-to-shrine assignment remains a systems/runtime concern.## Exceptional Direct Threshold Routes

The limited direct macro links from the Broken Dwarven Realm, Vent-Cities, and Shattered Heart now receive concrete upper connector nodes.

- `DR-H3 -> DR-CROWN1 -> CR-O1`: **Broken Sky Lift**. Requires restoration plus route knowledge; it bypasses the Caldera only after contestants commit to an upper dwarven connector before Day 52.
- `VC-S1 -> VC-CROWN1 -> CR-O2`: **Needle Chimney**. Requires Vent-City social access, a guide or equivalent route knowledge, and a difficult vertical traverse. Using Smoke Market as the origin prevents one Jungle Vent entrance from uniquely owning the shortcut.
- `SH-H4 -> SH-CROWN1 -> CR-O3`: **Crown Scar Traverse**. Exposed, dangerous, and easy for rivals to observe once used.

These routes reach existing threshold sectors rather than creating private qualification gates. They are exceptional route families, not superior versions of the normal Caldera approaches.## Consumption of Previous Reserved Interfaces

### Day 45

No additional inner-network hubs close. The purpose of the third contraction is to consume peripheral Jungle space and selected low/deep first-layer routes while leaving network-mastery geography available.

### Day 52

The Final Contraction removes most outer inner-network branches and leaves only upper/inner connectors, the Caldera, and the Crucible threshold network.

Close at Day 52:
- all VD, LA, EK, and SH landing nodes fed directly from the first mixing layer;
- `VD-XE1`, `VD-H1`, and `VD-H2`;
- `LA-XD1`, `LA-H1`, and `LA-H3`;
- `EK-H1` and `EK-H2`;
- `SH-XG1`, `SH-XG2`, `SH-H1`, and `SH-H2`.

Remain viable after Day 52:
- `VD-H3` Starfold Axis;
- `LA-H2` Returning Gallery;
- `EK-H3` Ember Stair;
- `SH-H3` Red Rift and `SH-H4` Crown Fracture;
- all Caldera landings, hubs, and threshold approaches;
- `DR-CROWN1`, `VC-CROWN1`, and `SH-CROWN1` if reached before their outer origin closes;
- all wilderness-facing Crucible threshold and shrine nodes.

This converts a broad network into several narrow but independent final route families rather than one corridor.## Final Route Families

At Day 52 the topology preserves more than the minimum three approach families:

1. **Elemental ascent:** `EK-H3 -> CU-EK2 -> CU-H2 -> CU-T2 -> CR-O2`.
2. **Fault ascent:** `SH-H3/H4 -> CU-SH2/S3 -> CU-H2/H3 -> CU-T2/T3 -> CR-O2/O3`.
3. **Veiled upper route:** `VD-H3 -> CU-VD1 -> CU-H3 -> CU-T3 -> CR-O3`.
4. **Labyrinth upper route:** `LA-H2 -> CU-LA1 -> CU-H1 -> CU-T1 -> CR-O1`.
5. **Exceptional bypasses:** Broken Sky Lift, Needle Chimney, and Crown Scar Traverse reach the same public outer threshold sectors without crossing the Caldera.

The Caldera triangle lets a team change threshold choice after entering the final wilderness. A rival controlling one causeway or outer gate therefore cannot control all qualification access.

## Fairness Constraints for Exceptional Starts

The three early unusual Jungle seeds remain useful but not dominant:
- `VD-E1` requires Blind Meridian plus at least two Veiled hub transitions before its shortest reliable final route.
- `LA-D1` enters through False Arrival and the unstable Labyrinth rather than a direct Caldera corridor.
- `SH-G1` crosses two fault-local nodes before reaching the outer Shattered Heart and still must traverse the collision mesh before any final approach.

Likewise, the direct DR and VC threshold bypasses require restoration/social access and prior route knowledge. Their existence rewards exploration and relationships rather than starting position alone.## Structural Validation Targets

The structured graph for this phase must demonstrate:
- all 31 previously reserved interfaces are consumed exactly once;
- no edge violates the structural-handoff macro connectivity matrix;
- the open inner network has no single mandatory pre-Crucible node;
- after Day 52, at least three independent threshold approaches remain reachable;
- removal or occupation of any one Caldera hub or one outer threshold does not eliminate qualification access;
- the five gem-recovery shrines remain reachable from multiple threshold sectors;
- exceptional direct routes do not create private or exclusive Crucible entrances.

Dungeondraft battlemap production should begin only after those priorities are accepted.## Validation Results

The structured graph `maps/topology/inner-convergence-v1.json` was validated against the preceding topology source.

Results:
- 83 unique nodes and 105 edges; no duplicate IDs or dangling edge endpoints;
- all 31 reserved interfaces from `interior-mixing-v1.json` are consumed exactly once;
- zero cross-area edges violate the canonical connectivity matrix;
- combining the first mixing layer with this graph produces a minimum entry-to-threshold node cut of 3: all three outer threshold sectors must be removed before global qualification access is severed;
- after Day 52, the surviving 33-node graph remains one connected component;
- every surviving inner route origin (`VD-H3`, `LA-H2`, `EK-H3`, `SH-H3`, `SH-H4`) can reach all three outer threshold sectors;
- loss of any one Caldera hub still leaves qualification access; loss of any one outer threshold leaves the other two reachable.

Reliable-route distance from each Jungle territory hub to its best threshold is 9-10 transitions. With all exceptional shortcuts assumed discovered and usable, best distances become 5-7 transitions from the territory hub; JR-E reaches 5 only through the conditional Broken Sky Lift. Including the earlier insertion feeder/hub/penetration transitions places that route inside the canonical 7-9-transition freakishly-lucky envelope rather than making it a normal advantage.
