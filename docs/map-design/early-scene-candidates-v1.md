# The Ashen Gauntlet: Early Scene Candidates V1

Status: Approved production-planning candidate slate. No player insertion territory is selected. Geographic names are provisional; topology bindings are stable.

## Selection Goal

Prepare scene concepts for JR-A, JR-B, JR-C and JR-F because each offers three ordinary route families and enough mechanical breadth for an unknown level-5 player composition.

The hub is the strongest reusable scene candidate in each territory because it expresses the campaign’s route-choice premise. A secondary traversal candidate is also identified so the eventual starting package can include exploration before the hub if useful.

## JR-A Candidate Pair

### `JR-A-H` — Raincoil Confluence

Role: wet-jungle hub connecting BK/GW/VE.

Spatial identity: two arrival shelves descend toward a broad basalt island split by braided mineral water. The three outward routes occupy different edges and elevations rather than three adjacent cave mouths.

Route previews: BK appears as half-drowned ancient masonry sinking below the waterline; GW is an oversized beast-cut bore with churned mud and bone traces; VE is a high root-choked green breach with condensation and oversized plant growth.

Why useful: strongest “the volcano contains different worlds” introduction while remaining broadly accessible to ordinary parties.

### `JR-A-O1` — Flooded Trail Forks

Role: early traversal before the hub, with optional access toward the drowned reward branch.

Spatial identity: shallow braided water, mangrove/root barriers, two usable dry shelves and evidence that an older stone alignment continues beneath silt.

Use if selected: provides a low-pressure first exploration map emphasizing route reading, water and cover rather than a mandatory opening combat.

## JR-B Candidate Pair

### `JR-B-H` — Crownbreak Shelf

Role: elevated hub connecting VE/DR/VC.

Spatial identity: a fractured basalt/canopy shelf wrapped around one massive fallen or living tree. One route stays lush and biological, one exposes square-cut dwarven stone in the cliff, and one narrows into a warm soot-marked vent throat.

Route previews: VE is living root/green architecture; DR is a broad engineered descent with broken masonry; VC is a narrow warm vertical route with airflow, soot and signs of small-scale habitation.

Why useful: excellent verticality and strong contrast between ecological, engineered and social route families.

### `JR-B-O2` — Hanging-Root Descent

Role: traversal node that can also receive contestants crossing from JR-A.

Spatial identity: broken canopy levels connected by giant roots and narrow basalt ledges, with partial views into lower jungle but no simple ground-level path.

## JR-C Candidate Pair

### `JR-C-H` — Blackwater Divide

Role: cataract/ravine hub connecting BK/DR/GW plus the dangerous surface crossing toward JR-D.

Spatial identity: terraced basalt around a deep water-cut divide. A waterfall or cataract creates both sound and visual separation while leaving multiple crossings and overlooks.

Route previews: BK descends through a flooded well-road with recognizable ancient paving; DR follows a straight drainage/service gallery; GW enters a violently water-cut burrow beneath or beside the cataract; the JR-D crossing is an exposed ash-ridge traverse visible above the ravine.

Why useful: the richest early hub for showing four different route choices and tactical elevation without requiring magical navigation.

### `JR-C-O1` — Cataract Shelf

Role: early traversal with strong sight/sound cues toward the hub.

Spatial identity: wet ledges, spray, fractured stone and partial ancient retaining work. Travel is horizontally close but vertically constrained.

## JR-F Candidate Pair

### `JR-F-H` — Ashfall Junction

Role: reclaimed lava-terrace hub connecting GW/VC/VE.

Spatial identity: broad cracked basalt terraces divided by ash gullies and younger vegetation. The hub should feel drier than JR-A/C but still visibly reclaimed by jungle.

Route previews: GW is a collapsed lava tube widened by creatures; VC is a narrow furnace-draft cleft with soot and worked footholds; VE is a lower green breach where roots and water have invaded an old lava fracture.

Why useful: balanced natural/social/ecological choice in a visually different, drier Jungle territory with strong volcanic identity.

### `JR-F-O1` — Cracked Lava Terrace

Role: early traversal with optional route toward the abandoned ridge cache.

Spatial identity: stepped lava plates, loose ash, scrub growth and fissures that create short tactical height changes without committing to a full cliff map.

## Candidate Comparison

| Hub | Strongest production value | Main complexity | Best if player party later needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| `JR-A-H` | strongest wet-jungle/world-layer introduction | water geometry | broad ordinary route choice and exploration |
| `JR-B-H` | strongest vertical and route-family contrast | canopy/elevation | mobility, social/hidden or engineered options |
| `JR-C-H` | richest four-way navigation and ravine tactics | waterfall/chasm readability | conventional routes with strong scouting value |
| `JR-F-H` | clearest volcanic-jungle identity | terrace elevation | broad choice without wet-jungle dominance |

No candidate is designated as the player start. Exact insertion selection remains a later Game Start Manager decision after player capabilities are known.
